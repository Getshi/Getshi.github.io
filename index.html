I am currently working as a research scientist at CLO Virtual Fashion.

Before that, I received my BSc and MSc in Visual Computing at the Technical University of Vienna. In 2022, I finished my PhD in physics-based simulation under the supervision of Chris Wojtan at the Institute of Science and Technology Austria.

My research interests include physics-based animation of various natural phenomena (cloth, yarns, granular media, fluids, ...), multi-scale simulation, and knitted textiles.
